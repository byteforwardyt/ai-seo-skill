# Local search

Use for Google Business Profile, Apple Business Connect, Bing Places, and the citations and reviews around them.

Primary docs:

- https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177
- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/establish-business-details
- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/local-business
- https://businessconnect.apple.com/

## What actually moves a local listing

The map result and the web result are different systems. Fix the one the user is missing.

On the Business Profile:

- The primary category should be the service a searcher would type, not a clever brand category. Additional categories only if they are true.
- Name, address, phone, and hours must match the website and the door. Keyword-stuffing the business name violates Google's representation policy.
- Services, service area, and opening hours should be complete. A service-area business should not publish a fake storefront address.
- Photos should show the real place, work, and team. Stock photos of a different business are a trust problem.
- The website link should land on the location or service page, not a blocked or irrelevant URL.
- Posts and products are optional. They do not replace a wrong category.

On the website:

- A unique page for each real location, with the address, hours, and the services offered there.
- LocalBusiness markup that matches that page.
- Embedded map and driving information if a person needs to visit. Not a ranking trick. A usability fix.
- City pages only for cities the business actually serves, with something true about that city. A swapped city name on a template is a doorway page.

## Citations and reviews

The same name, address, and phone on the major directories the business can claim. Fix the big inconsistencies before paying for a citation blast.

Reviews: ask customers who used the service. Do not gate reviews, offer incentives Google forbids, or review the business from staff accounts. Review velocity is not a lever to manufacture. A sudden cluster of similar reviews is a risk, not a tactic.

Respond to reviews as the business. Do not argue medical or legal outcomes in a reply.

## Apple and Bing

Claim Apple Business Connect and Bing Places if those results matter in the market. They do not copy Google's profile automatically. Check the name, category, and hours in each.

## What not to sell

Do not promise a map-pack position from a number of citations, a star rating, or a photo count. Say which field is wrong, and what the profile or the location page should show instead.
